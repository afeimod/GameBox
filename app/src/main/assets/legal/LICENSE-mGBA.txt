Libretro port
-------------
The libretro port (libretro.c) is included in the mGBA source tree
(src/platform/libretro/) and compiled as a static library via mGBA's
own CMake, then linked with NesStation's JNI bridge — see
core/cmake/CMakeLists.txt for build configuration.
